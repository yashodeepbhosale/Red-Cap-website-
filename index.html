About Red Cap

Red Cap is a tech-driven platform aimed at modernizing Indian railway sahayak (coolie) services. With digital booking, transparent pricing, luggage tracking, and welfare-driven policies, Red Cap is making travel easier, fairer, and more secure for millions of passengers across India.

About the Founder

Yashodeep Bhosale, the visionary founder of Red Cap, is a dynamic and innovative mind driven by a passion for transforming traditional systems through smart technology. Currently pursuing a degree in Electronics and Telecommunications Engineering, Yashodeep combines technical expertise with a deep understanding of real-world challenges faced by everyday commuters.

With Red Cap, he aims to revolutionize the Indian railway experience by digitizing coolie (sahayak) services, promoting fair wages, and ensuring luggage security through a user-friendly mobile platform. His commitment to social impact, efficient design, and tech-driven solutions forms the backbone of Red Cap’s mission.
